# AML/KYC Policy

**OMAYA EXPRESS EXCHANGE**

**Effective Date:** 31 May 2026  
**Last Updated:** 31 May 2026  
**Version:** 1.1

---

This AML/KYC Policy describes how **OMAYA EXPRESS EXCHANGE** (trading as **OMAYA.io**, "**OMAYA**," "**we**," "**us**," or "**our**") prevents money laundering, terrorism financing, and other financial crime, and how we verify the identity of our customers. It is a public summary of OMAYA's internal Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing (AML/CFT) Policies and Procedures and forms part of our **Terms of Service**. It applies to all users of our website, mobile application, and services (the "**Services**").

Because financial crime controls must remain effective, this Policy describes our approach at a general level and does not disclose the internal thresholds, scoring rules, detection scenarios, or procedures that would undermine those controls.

---

## 1. Our Commitment

OMAYA has **zero tolerance** for money laundering, terrorism financing, proliferation financing, sanctions evasion, fraud, and other financial crime. We are committed to operating with integrity and in full compliance with applicable law, and we apply a **risk-based approach** to identifying, assessing, mitigating, and managing these risks.

## 2. Regulatory Status and Framework

OMAYA holds a Commercial Licence issued by the Ministry of Commerce, a licence from the Central Bank of Somalia, and a licence from the Banadir Regional Administration.

Our AML/CFT program is designed to comply with applicable **Somali law**, and we align our program with recognised **international standards**, including the recommendations of the Financial Action Task Force (FATF).

## 3. Governance and Oversight

- our **Board of Directors** has ultimate oversight and approves our AML/CFT policy;
- we appoint a **Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO)**; and
- we operate a "**three lines of defence**" model.

## 4. Risk-Based Approach and Customer Risk Rating

We classify customers and relationships into risk categories (for example, **low, standard, medium, high, or prohibited**) based on factors including customer type, geography, product use, transaction behaviour, sanctions and PEP exposure, source of funds and source of wealth, and delivery channel.

## 5. Customer Due Diligence (KYC)

Depending on risk, we apply **Simplified**, **Standard**, or **Enhanced** due diligence.

As part of KYC, we may require:

- **Identity information**
- **Identity documents**
- **Verification checks** (document-authenticity, facial-image, liveness checks)
- **Purpose and activity information**
- **Source of funds and source of wealth** information

## 6. Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)

We apply EDD to higher-risk customers and activity.

## 7. Sanctions Compliance

We screen customers and transactions against applicable sanctions lists and may freeze assets or block transactions where required.

## 8. Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)

We screen for PEPs and apply EDD and senior-management approval where relevant.

## 9. Transaction Monitoring

We monitor activity to detect unusual transactions and potential financial crime and may reject, delay, or investigate transactions as needed.

## 10. Digital Asset and Crypto Compliance

We use **blockchain-analytics tools** to screen wallet addresses and transactions, and we may apply controls to transfers involving **self-hosted wallets** and comply with **Travel Rule** requirements.

## 11. Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Controls

We apply additional controls to P2P trading and may monitor P2P chat and evidence for compliance and fraud purposes.

## 12. Reliance on Third Parties and Partner Due Diligence

We perform risk-based due diligence on partners (payment providers, banks, brokers, and other service providers).

## 13. Suspicious Activity and Regulatory Reporting

Where required, we report suspicious activity to the **Financial Reporting Center (FRC)** and cooperate with authorities. We may be legally prohibited from informing you of reports or investigations ("tipping off").

## 14. Prohibited Customers and Activities

We do not establish or maintain relationships with sanctioned persons, anonymous customers, shell banks, or customers who refuse required information.

## 15. Record-Keeping

We retain records for the periods required by applicable law (generally **at least five (5) years**).

## 16. Account Restrictions, Refusal, and Closure

We may refuse onboarding, restrict features, delay or freeze transactions, or close accounts to comply with law and manage risk.

## 17. Your Responsibilities

You must provide accurate information, complete verification, use payment accounts in your own name (unless approved otherwise), and cooperate with compliance checks.

## 18. Program Governance, Training and Review

We train relevant personnel, apply controls, and test and review our program periodically.

## 19. Changes to This Policy

We may update this AML/KYC Policy from time to time.
