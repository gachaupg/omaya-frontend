## 1. Personal Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data.

### 1.1 Information you provide to us

- **Identity and contact information:** full name, date and place of birth, nationality, residential address, email address, and phone number.
- **Identity verification (KYC) information:** identity documents (such as passport, national identity card, or driving licence), proof of address, and related photographs or scans.
- **Biometric and liveness data:** a facial image or selfie, liveness-check data, and document-authenticity data, collected through our identity-verification provider to confirm your identity and prevent fraud. These checks are used only for identity verification, fraud prevention, and compliance. Where biometric verification is required for KYC, refusing it may prevent us from opening or maintaining your Account or providing certain Services. Our verification provider may process this information on our behalf, and OMAYA generally receives verification results, risk indicators, and supporting records rather than raw biometric templates. Where we rely on your consent for biometric processing, we seek it separately at the point of verification (see Section 1.4).
- **Financial and transaction information:** payment details (including, where you make card payments, limited payment-card information processed by our payment providers — we do not store full card details), bank and mobile-money account details, wallet addresses, transaction history, and information about your source of funds and source of wealth.
- **Account and profile information:** username, account settings, and preferences.
- **P2P information:** advertisements, order details, in-app chat messages, payment proofs, and dispute evidence. If you are a P2P Merchant, we may also collect additional verification information, security-deposit or reserve details, and performance metrics such as completion and release rates.
- **Communications:** messages to our support team, complaints, and survey or feedback responses.
- **Corporate account information:** entity details, directors, beneficial owners, authorised users, and supporting documents.
- **Privacy choices and consent records:** records of your consents, permissions, opt-outs, and withdrawals, such as for marketing, cookies, location, and biometric processing.

### 1.2 Information we collect automatically

- **Device and technical information:** device and browser type, operating system, device identifiers, and app version.
- **Network and location information:** IP address, approximate location, and, where you grant permission, more precise location.
- **Usage information:** features and pages used, log data, and session and login activity.
- **Security and fraud-prevention signals:** device fingerprint, risk signals, and behavioural patterns used to detect fraud and protect accounts.
- **Diagnostics and notifications:** analytics, crash reports, diagnostics, performance and error logs, and, where you enable push notifications, device tokens and notification delivery information.
- **Cookies and similar technologies:** as described in our **Cookie Policy**.

### 1.3 Blockchain and transaction data

Digital Asset transactions are recorded on public blockchains. We may collect and analyse wallet addresses and on-chain transaction data, including by using blockchain-analytics tools, for compliance, security, and fraud-prevention purposes.

### 1.4 Information we receive from third parties

We may receive personal data about you from: identity-verification and screening providers (including **Sumsub**, which performs identity verification, biometric and document checks, and sanctions, PEP, and watchlist screening); sanctions, politically-exposed-person, and adverse-media data sources; blockchain-analytics providers; payment, banking, and mobile-money partners; third-party sign-in providers (where you choose to register or log in using them); our affiliates; and publicly available sources.

## 2. How We Use Your Personal Data

We use your personal data to:

- provide, operate, and maintain the Services and process your Transactions;
- create, verify, and manage your Account, including identity verification and onboarding;
- meet our anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorism-financing obligations, including KYC, sanctions and PEP screening, transaction monitoring, and the Travel Rule;
- detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, scams, and security threats, and manage risk;
- comply with our legal and regulatory obligations and respond to requests from regulators, the Financial Reporting Center, the Central Bank of Somalia, and other competent authorities;
- secure the Platform and your Account;
- provide customer support and handle complaints;
- improve, personalise, and develop the Services;
- send you service, transaction, security, and legal communications, and, where permitted, marketing communications; and
- enforce our Terms of Service and protect our rights, property, and users.

## 3. Legal Bases for Processing

Where data-protection law (such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation) applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

- **Performance of a contract** — to provide the Services you request and operate your Account.
- **Compliance with legal obligations** — including anti-money-laundering, counter-terrorism-financing, sanctions, tax, and record-keeping requirements.
- **Legitimate interests** — including securing our Platform, preventing fraud, improving the Services, and direct marketing where permitted, balanced against your rights and interests.
- **Consent** — for certain marketing, certain cookies, and where otherwise required; you may withdraw consent at any time.
- **Biometric and special-category data** — we process biometric data (for identity verification) where it is necessary for compliance with our legal obligations and/or on the basis of your explicit consent, and on grounds of substantial public interest where applicable.

The table below maps our main processing purposes to their legal bases. Where more than one basis applies, we rely on each as relevant:

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Creating and operating your Account | Performance of a contract |
| KYC, AML, sanctions and PEP screening | Legal obligation; substantial public interest; explicit consent where required (e.g., biometrics) |
| Processing Transactions | Performance of a contract; legal obligation |
| Fraud prevention and security | Legitimate interests; legal obligation |
| Travel Rule compliance | Legal obligation |
| Customer support | Performance of a contract; legitimate interests |
| Marketing | Consent, or legitimate interests where permitted |
| Improving the Services | Legitimate interests |
| Legal claims and enforcement | Legitimate interests; legal obligation |

## 4. How We Share Your Personal Data

**We do not sell your personal data.** We may share it with:

- **Service providers and processors** acting on our behalf under contract, including identity-verification and screening providers (such as Sumsub), hosting and IT providers, analytics and communications providers, and customer-support providers;
- **Payment, banking, and mobile-money partners, liquidity providers, and cryptocurrency exchanges**, to process or route your Transactions;
- **Partner foreign exchange Brokers**, where you use our Forex Services, to facilitate your onboarding, compliance vetting, and the settlement of local deposits and withdrawals;
- **Blockchain-analytics and fraud-prevention providers**;
- **Other virtual asset service providers and financial institutions**, where necessary to comply with the Travel Rule or to process a transfer. To comply with the Travel Rule, we may be required to transmit information such as the originator's name, account or wallet identifier, and address or identification number, together with the beneficiary's name and account or wallet identifier, to the counterparty provider;
- **Regulators, the Financial Reporting Center, the Central Bank of Somalia, law enforcement, courts, and other authorities**, where required or permitted by law;
- **Professional advisers**, such as legal, audit, and accounting advisers;
- **Parties to a corporate transaction**, such as a merger, acquisition, or reorganisation; and
- **Other parties with your consent** or at your direction.

We require our service providers to protect your personal data and to use it only for the purposes for which it is shared. For more information about how we handle law-enforcement and government requests, see our **Law Enforcement Request Guidelines**.

We are based in Somalia and may transfer and store personal data in Somalia and in other countries where we or our service providers operate. Our service providers — including our identity-verification provider, hosting and analytics providers, blockchain-analytics providers, and payment partners — may process personal data in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and other countries where they or their sub-processors operate. You may contact us at **privacy@omaya.io** to request further information about the safeguards we use for international transfers.

We use automated systems and automated decision-making for fraud detection, transaction and sanctions screening, risk scoring, and compliance. These processes may result in a Transaction or Account being delayed, refused, restricted, or closed. Where a decision that produces legal or similarly significant effects is based solely on automated processing, you may request human review — except where the processing is necessary for entering into or performing a contract with you, is required for compliance with our legal obligations (such as anti-money-laundering requirements), or is based on your explicit consent.

## 10. Data Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy and to meet our legal, regulatory, and record-keeping obligations. Indicative retention periods are:

- **Identity, KYC, and transaction records:** at least five (5) years after the end of your relationship with us or after the relevant Transaction, as required by anti-money-laundering and record-keeping rules.
- **Compliance, screening, and suspicious-activity records:** at least five (5) years.
- **Account and communications records:** for the duration of your relationship with us and a reasonable period afterward.
- **Marketing data:** until you withdraw consent or object, or until it is no longer needed.
- **Biometric and liveness records:** retained only for as long as necessary for identity verification, fraud prevention, AML/CFT compliance, legal claims, and audit, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.
- **Security, device, and fraud-prevention logs:** for the period necessary for security and fraud-prevention purposes.

After the applicable period, we delete or anonymise your personal data, subject to copies that may remain in backups for a limited period until overwritten (see Section 13).

## 11. Data Security

We maintain technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data, including encryption, access controls, and monitoring. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a personal-data breach, we will assess it promptly and, where required by applicable law (including the Data Protection Act, 2023), notify affected users and/or the competent authority.

## 12. Your Privacy Rights

The following rights apply to the extent required by the data-protection law that applies to you, and may vary depending on where you live:

- **Access** — to obtain confirmation of, and a copy of, the personal data we hold about you;
- **Rectification** — to correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
- **Erasure** — to request deletion of your data (the "right to be forgotten");
- **Restriction** — to restrict our processing in certain circumstances;
- **Data portability** — to receive certain data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format;
- **Objection** — to object to processing based on legitimate interests and to direct marketing;
- **Withdrawal of consent** — where processing is based on consent;
- **Automated decisions** — not to be subject to solely automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects, subject to the exceptions in Section 7; and
- **Complaint** — to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

These rights are subject to limitations. In particular, we are required to retain certain records (such as KYC and transaction records) for anti-money-laundering, tax, and legal purposes, and may be unable to delete them even if you request erasure.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **privacy@omaya.io**. We may need to verify your identity before responding. We will respond within the time required by applicable law (for example, within one month under the GDPR, which may be extended for complex requests).

## 13. Account Closure and Data Deletion

You may close your Account as described in our Terms of Service. You may also request deletion of your Account and personal data:

- through the **in-app account-deletion option** in the OMAYA.io mobile application; or
- through our **account-deletion page** at https://omaya.io/delete-account, which is also linked in the App and on our website.

Account closure and account deletion are different. Closure ends your access to the Services; deletion involves removing or deactivating personal data from our active systems. Following a deletion request, we will delete or anonymise your personal data, **except** for data we are required or permitted to retain for the periods set out in Section 10 (including KYC, transaction, compliance, tax, and legal records). Deletion may be delayed or refused where your Account has pending Transactions, remaining balances, unresolved disputes, investigations, or legal holds. Copies of deleted data may remain in our backups and disaster-recovery systems for a limited period until they are overwritten in the ordinary course. If those systems are ever restored, we apply controls designed to ensure that data you asked us to delete is not reinstated into our active systems and is re-deleted.

## 14. Children's Privacy

The Services are intended only for individuals aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a person under 18, we will delete it.
