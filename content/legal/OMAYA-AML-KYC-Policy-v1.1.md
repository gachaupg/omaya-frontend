# AML/KYC Policy

**OMAYA EXPRESS EXCHANGE**

**Effective Date:** 31 May 2026  
**Last Updated:** 31 May 2026  
**Version:** 1.1

---

This AML/KYC Policy describes how **OMAYA EXPRESS EXCHANGE** (trading as **OMAYA.io**, "**OMAYA**," "**we**," "**us**," or "**our**") prevents money laundering, terrorism financing, and other financial crime, and how we verify the identity of our customers. It is a public summary of OMAYA's internal Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing (AML/CFT) Policies and Procedures and forms part of our **Terms of Service**. It applies to all users of our website, mobile application, and services (the "**Services**").

Because financial crime controls must remain effective, this Policy describes our approach at a general level and does not disclose the internal thresholds, scoring rules, detection scenarios, or procedures that would undermine those controls.

---

## 1. Our Commitment

OMAYA has **zero tolerance** for money laundering, terrorism financing, proliferation financing, sanctions evasion, fraud, and other financial crime. We are committed to operating with integrity and in full compliance with applicable law, and we apply a **risk-based approach** to identifying, assessing, mitigating, and managing these risks across all of our products, customers, channels, and the jurisdictions in which we operate. We will not establish or maintain relationships with sanctioned parties, shell banks, or anonymous customers, and we will not knowingly facilitate any unlawful transaction.

## 2. Regulatory Status and Framework

OMAYA holds a Commercial Licence issued by the Ministry of Commerce, a licence from the Central Bank of Somalia, and a licence from the Banadir Regional Administration. OMAYA provides its Services only within the scope of its licences, registrations, permissions, exemptions, or approved partnerships.

Our AML/CFT program is designed to comply with applicable **Somali law**, including the Anti-Money Laundering Law, the Counter-Terrorism Financing Law, the regulations of the Financial Reporting Center (FRC), the directives of the Central Bank of Somalia, and Company Law No. 18 of 2019. We also align our program with recognised **international standards**, including the recommendations of the Financial Action Task Force (FATF), the Wolfsberg Group principles for correspondent banking, guidance of the Basel Committee, and applicable United Nations Security Council Resolutions.

## 3. Governance and Oversight

Responsibility for our AML/CFT program is embedded across the organisation:

- our **Board of Directors** has ultimate oversight and approves our AML/CFT policy;
- we appoint a **Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO)** of appropriate seniority and independence, who oversees the program and is the primary point of contact with competent authorities; and
- we operate a "**three lines of defence**" model, with business teams, an independent compliance function, and independent testing and audit.

We allocate appropriate resources, training, and technology to keep the program effective.

## 4. Risk-Based Approach and Customer Risk Rating

We conduct an enterprise-wide risk assessment and apply controls that are proportionate to risk. We consider risk factors relating to the **customer** (for example, customer type, ownership, and politically-exposed-person status), the **product or service**, the **geography** (including higher-risk and sanctioned jurisdictions and jurisdictions identified by the FATF), the **delivery channel** (including remote onboarding), and the **transaction**.

We classify customers and relationships into risk categories — such as **low, standard, medium, high, or prohibited** — based on factors including customer type, geography, product use, transaction behaviour, sanctions and PEP exposure, source of funds and source of wealth, and delivery channel. Higher-risk relationships and activity are subject to enhanced measures (see Section 6). We do not publish the detailed scoring rules or thresholds we apply.

We monitor sanctions lists, FATF high-risk and monitored jurisdictions, and other country-risk sources, and may update restrictions, limits, and due-diligence requirements when those sources change.

## 5. Customer Due Diligence (KYC)

**Levels of due diligence.** Depending on the assessed risk, we apply different levels of due diligence: **Simplified Due Diligence** for lower-risk customers where permitted by law; **Standard Due Diligence** for most customers; and **Enhanced Due Diligence** for higher-risk customers (Section 6).

**Verification tiers and limits.** Access to certain Services and to higher transaction, deposit, and withdrawal limits depends on the level of verification you have completed. Unlocking higher limits or additional features may require additional verification. The applicable limits are shown on the Platform.

As part of Customer Due Diligence (CDD), also known as Know Your Customer (KYC), we may require you to provide and allow us to verify:

- **Identity information** — such as your full name, date of birth, nationality, and residential address;
- **Identity documents** — such as a passport, national identity card, or driving licence, and proof of address;
- **Verification checks** — including document-authenticity, facial-image, and liveness checks carried out through specialist third-party providers, to confirm your identity and detect fraud;
- **Purpose and activity information** — the intended purpose of your relationship with us and your expected transaction activity; and
- **Source of funds and source of wealth** information, particularly for higher-value or higher-risk relationships.

**Business and corporate customers.** For legal-entity customers, we additionally verify the entity, its ownership and control structure, its directors and authorised representatives, and its **beneficial owners** and controlling persons, in accordance with applicable law and our risk-based procedures — including persons who directly or indirectly own or control the entity, who exercise control through other means, or who act as senior managing officials where no other natural person is identified.

**Ongoing monitoring and rescreening.** We carry out ongoing monitoring of your relationship and transactions and keep your information up to date. We may rescreen customers, beneficial owners, counterparties, wallet addresses, and transactions periodically and when risk events occur — including document expiry, profile changes, unusual activity, sanctions-list updates, changes in PEP status, adverse media, jurisdiction changes, or changes in ownership or control. We may request additional information or documentation at any time, and you must provide it promptly.

## 6. Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)

We apply **Enhanced Due Diligence** to higher-risk customers and activity. This may include customers and transactions connected to higher-risk or sanctioned jurisdictions, politically exposed persons and their close associates, complex or opaque ownership structures, unusual or high-value activity, and other higher-risk indicators. EDD may involve obtaining additional identity, source-of-funds, and source-of-wealth evidence, senior-management approval of the relationship, and enhanced ongoing monitoring.

## 7. Sanctions Compliance

We screen customers, beneficial owners, related parties, and transactions against applicable sanctions lists, including those administered by the **United Nations**, the **United States Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC)**, the **European Union**, the **United Kingdom**, and the **Central Bank of Somalia**, as well as other applicable lists. We do not deal with sanctioned persons or comprehensively sanctioned territories. Where required by sanctions law or by the direction of a competent authority, we may **freeze assets, block or reject transactions, restrict or close accounts, and report the matter, in each case without prior notice**.

## 8. Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)

We screen for **politically exposed persons (PEPs)** and their family members and close associates, both at onboarding and on an ongoing basis. Relationships involving PEPs are treated as higher-risk and are subject to Enhanced Due Diligence and senior-management approval.

## 9. Transaction Monitoring

We monitor activity on the Platform, using a combination of automated tools and trained staff, to detect activity that is unusual or that may indicate money laundering, terrorism financing, fraud, or other financial crime. We may **reject, delay, or investigate** transactions involving third-party payments, mismatched account names, payment splitting, mule-account indicators, circular flows, unexplained pass-through activity, or activity inconsistent with your profile.

We apply the **same AML scrutiny to mobile money transfers as to bank transfers**. Where you send or receive payments through a bank or mobile money account, that account must be registered in the same name and to the same identity as your OMAYA Account, unless we have approved otherwise. Where activity requires review, we may request further information, delay or decline a transaction, or take other appropriate action.

## 10. Digital Asset and Crypto Compliance

For Digital Asset activity, we apply additional controls appropriate to the sector. We use **blockchain-analytics tools** to screen wallet addresses and transactions, and we assess wallet and transaction risk across higher-risk categories — including sanctioned wallets, darknet markets, ransomware, scams or stolen funds, mixing or tumbling services, high-risk or unlicensed exchanges, gambling services, privacy coins or privacy-enhancing services, certain self-hosted wallets, and newly created or otherwise unusual wallets. We may **block, delay, reject, freeze, return, or report** transfers connected to these risks.

We may require verification that you own or control an external wallet and apply enhanced checks to transfers involving **self-hosted wallets**. We collect and transmit originator and beneficiary information for Digital Asset transfers **at or above applicable regulatory thresholds** when transacting with other virtual asset service providers (the "**Travel Rule**"). Before or while transacting with another virtual asset service provider, we may assess that provider's identity, jurisdiction, regulatory status, sanctions exposure, AML/CFT controls, and ability to securely receive and transmit Travel Rule information.

## 11. Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Controls

Peer-to-peer trading carries specific financial-crime risks, and we apply additional controls. Payment accounts used in P2P trades must be registered in your own name and match your verified identity; **third-party payments are not permitted unless we approve them**. P2P Merchants and high-volume traders may be subject to Enhanced Due Diligence, security deposits or reserves, limits, and enhanced monitoring. We may monitor P2P chat and payment evidence for compliance and fraud purposes, and may review, hold, or restrict trades and accounts in response to repeated cancellations, false or altered receipts, chargebacks, attempts to settle off-platform, or other higher-risk activity.

## 12. Reliance on Third Parties and Partner Due Diligence

We conduct risk-based due diligence on relevant partners — including payment providers, banks, mobile money providers, liquidity providers, foreign exchange brokers, and other virtual asset service providers — before and during the relationship, and we expect them to maintain AML/CFT standards consistent with applicable law. Where we act as a **local agent for a foreign exchange broker**, you also complete the broker's own verification; we and the broker each apply customer due diligence appropriate to our respective roles, and we do not rely solely on a third party where the law requires us to perform our own checks.

## 13. Suspicious Activity and Regulatory Reporting

Where we have reasonable grounds to suspect that activity is connected to financial crime, we are legally required to report it to the **Financial Reporting Center (FRC)** of the Federal Government of Somalia. Where required by law, we also report **large cash transactions, threshold-based transactions, and other reportable activity** to the FRC or competent authority. We cooperate fully with the FRC, the Central Bank of Somalia, law enforcement, and other competent authorities.

**By law, we may be prohibited from informing you that a report has been made or that an investigation is taking place ("tipping off"), and we may be unable to explain why a transaction or Account has been delayed, refused, frozen, or closed.** Our staff are required to keep AML/CFT reviews, reports, investigations, and law-enforcement requests confidential, including obligations not to tip off customers or third parties. Further information about how we handle official requests is set out in our **Law Enforcement Request Guidelines**.

## 14. Prohibited Customers and Activities

We do not establish or maintain relationships with, and we prohibit the use of the Services by or for:

- sanctioned persons or entities, or persons acting on their behalf;
- shell banks, and anonymous, fictitious, or numbered accounts;
- customers who refuse to provide the information or documentation we require; and
- any person using the Services for money laundering, terrorism financing, sanctions evasion, fraud, or any other prohibited activity.

The prohibited uses and prohibited business categories set out in our **Terms of Service** also apply.

## 15. Record-Keeping

We retain customer-identification, transaction, screening, and compliance records for the periods required by applicable law — generally **at least five (5) years** after the end of the relationship or the relevant transaction — and we store them securely. Our handling of personal data is described in our **Privacy Policy**.

## 16. Account Restrictions, Refusal, and Closure

To meet our legal obligations and manage financial crime risk, we may **refuse to open an Account, request additional information, delay, refuse, freeze, reverse, or cancel a transaction, restrict features, or suspend or close an Account**, in each case to the extent permitted or required by applicable law, and without prior notice where the law or the circumstances require.

If you believe a restriction, freeze, refusal, or closure has been applied in error, you may contact us and provide supporting documentation, such as additional source-of-funds or source-of-wealth evidence. We will consider it, but — as noted in Section 13 — we may be legally prohibited from explaining the reason for a restriction or report, or from confirming whether one has been made.

## 17. Your Responsibilities

When you use the Services, you must:

- provide accurate, current, and complete information, and keep it up to date;
- complete identity verification and provide any documentation we request;
- use only payment accounts registered in your own name, unless we have approved otherwise;
- use the Services only for lawful purposes and only for your own benefit, unless we have approved otherwise; and
- cooperate with our compliance checks and reasonable requests.

Failure to do so may result in your Account or transactions being delayed, restricted, refused, or closed, and may be reported to the authorities where required.

## 18. Program Governance, Training and Review

We provide AML/CFT training to relevant personnel and keep it current. We apply appropriate **employee screening, access controls, segregation of duties, and escalation procedures** for staff involved in onboarding, compliance, payments, and transaction review. Our program is subject to **independent testing and audit**, and we **review and update it periodically** and whenever there are material changes in law, products, risks, jurisdictions, typologies, or our operations.

## 19. Changes to This Policy

We may update this AML/KYC Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on the Platform and update the "Last Updated" date. Where the changes are material, we will take reasonable steps to notify you.
