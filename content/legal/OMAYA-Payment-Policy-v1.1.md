This Policy covers the fees and charges that apply to the Services, how exchange rates are applied, how deposits and withdrawals are processed, and how refunds, cancellations, chargebacks, and similar matters are handled. The specific fees, spreads, limits, and supported payment methods that apply to you are shown on the Platform at the time of your transaction and may be set out in a separate fee schedule (for example, at omaya.io/fees).

You agree to pay all applicable fees, spreads, commissions, and charges for the Services. Depending on the Service, these may include exchange or trading fees, Swap fees, MoneyX and transfer fees, remittance fees, deposit and withdrawal fees, currency-conversion margins, card-processing fees, and administrative fees. Fees may be charged as a fixed amount, a percentage, a spread or margin built into the rate we quote, or a combination of these, and are displayed or otherwise made available to you before or at the time you confirm a transaction.

We may **change our fees at any time**. Changes take effect when published, and your continued use of the Services after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised fees. Except as required by law or as expressly stated in this Policy, **fees, spreads, and commissions are non-refundable**, including where a transaction is later reversed or refunded for reasons attributable to you.

## 3. Exchange Rates and Quotes

Exchange rates and quotes are set by reference to market conditions and include our spread or margin. Rates and quotes are **indicative until a transaction is confirmed**, and the applicable rate is the rate confirmed at the time of the transaction. A quote may **expire** if the transaction is not completed within the required time, if market rates move, or if the transaction is subject to compliance review, in which case we may re-quote, cancel, or decline it.

## 4. Network (Blockchain) Fees

Digital Asset transactions are subject to **blockchain network fees** (sometimes called "gas" or "miner" fees), which are determined by the relevant network and not by OMAYA. Network fees can change rapidly and may be **deducted from the amount you send or receive**, or charged in addition to our fees. You are responsible for network fees, and they are generally **non-refundable**, including on any return or refund of Digital Assets (see Section 10).

## 5. Payment Methods, Ownership, and Limits

We make various payment methods available, which may include local banks, mobile money services, e-wallets, Digital Assets, and — where offered — debit or credit cards. The available methods are shown on the Platform and may be added, restricted, or removed at any time. Many payment methods involve third-party providers, whose processing times and rules are outside our control.

Unless we approve otherwise, any payment account you use (such as a bank or mobile money account) must be **registered in your own name and match your verified identity**. Payments from or to **third-party accounts, mismatched names, business or agent accounts, or unverified sources** may be **delayed, rejected, returned, frozen, or subject to enhanced due diligence**.

**Limits.** Minimum and maximum transaction limits may vary by Service, payment method, asset, network, Account type, KYC level, risk rating, jurisdiction, and operational availability, and are shown on the Platform.

## 6. Deposits

Deposits are credited to your Account after we have received and, where applicable, confirmed the funds — for Digital Assets, after the required number of network confirmations. A payment is treated as **final only when OMAYA determines that it has been received, reconciled, cleared, and is not subject to reversal, recall, chargeback, provider rejection, or fraud review**; a receipt, screenshot, or notification does not by itself prove final receipt. Some payment methods are processed only during business hours or before applicable **cut-off times**, and transactions submitted afterward may be processed on the next business day or when the relevant third-party provider is available. We may place a **hold on, delay, or decline** a deposit for compliance, security, fraud-prevention, or legal reasons.

## 7. Withdrawals

Withdrawals are subject to verification, limits, fees, and applicable network fees, and may be **delayed, held, or declined** for compliance, security, fraud-prevention, or legal reasons. For Digital Asset withdrawals, you must provide the **correct destination address and select the correct network**. Processing times are not guaranteed and may be affected by verification, network conditions, cut-off times, and third-party providers.

## 8. Your Responsibility for Transaction Details

**You are solely responsible for the accuracy of all transaction details that you provide**, including wallet addresses, blockchain networks, bank and mobile money account numbers, account names, payment references, beneficiary details, and amounts.

- **Digital Assets.** Blockchain transfers are **final and generally irreversible**. If you send Digital Assets to a **wrong or incompatible address**, select the **wrong network**, or send an **unsupported asset or token**, the assets may be **permanently lost**. OMAYA may be **unable to recover them, and cannot refund assets that it is unable to recover.** It is your responsibility to verify the asset, network, and address are correct before you send.
- **Fiat and local payments.** If you provide incorrect or mismatched payment details, your payment may fail, be delayed, be sent to the wrong recipient, or be impossible to reverse.

OMAYA is not responsible for any loss arising from incorrect, incomplete, outdated, or mismatched details that you provide.

## 9. Underpayment, Overpayment, Late, and Split Payments

If you **underpay, overpay, pay after a quote has expired, split a single payment into multiple payments, or omit a required payment reference**, we may delay, cancel, re-quote, partially process, or refund the net amount (after applicable fees), or request additional information, in each case acting reasonably and subject to compliance review.

## 10. Refunds

10.1. **General.** Completed transactions are generally **not refundable**. Refunds are made only where required by law, where we expressly agree, or as set out in this Section.

10.2. **Mistakes you make.** Where a transaction cannot be completed, or funds cannot be recovered, because of incorrect details you provided, we are **not obliged to refund** you, and any refund is subject to the deductions below and to our ability to recover the funds.

10.3. **Digital Assets sent in error.** If you send Digital Assets to a wrong address or network, or send an unsupported asset, the transfer is generally **irreversible and may be permanently lost**. OMAYA has **no obligation to attempt recovery and cannot refund what it cannot recover.** Where OMAYA does agree to attempt a recovery or return, recovery is not guaranteed, and OMAYA will deduct the relevant **network fees and any costs incurred** and may charge an **administrative fee**; any applicable recovery or administrative fee will be **shown in the fee schedule or communicated to you before recovery is attempted**.

10.4. **Refunds or returns of Digital Assets.** Where any refund or return of Digital Assets is made, we will deduct the applicable **network fees and any costs we incur**, and return only the net amount.

10.5. **Refunds or returns of fiat where you made the mistake.** Where we return fiat funds that you sent in error, or that could not be processed because of incorrect details you provided, we will deduct **all applicable commissions, fees, charges, and any exchange-rate difference or cost we incur**, and return only the **net amount**. Where a currency conversion is involved, the return is made at the **prevailing exchange rate**, which may be less favourable than the original rate.

10.6. **How and when refunds are made.** Where a refund is due, we will normally return funds to the **original source** where possible, after any verification we require. Refunds, where approved, may take **several business days or longer**, depending on the payment method, network, third-party provider, and any compliance review, and on whether the refund involves Digital Assets, cards, banks, or mobile money.

## 11. Cancellations

Once you confirm a transaction, it is your authorisation for us to proceed, and Digital Asset and many other transactions **cannot be cancelled** once submitted. We may **cancel, void, re-price, or decline** a transaction where required for compliance, security, or legal reasons, where a quote has expired, or where it was affected by a manifest or system error (see Section 9.5 of our Terms of Service).

Initiating a chargeback, recall, or payment dispute **without a valid, lawful basis is a breach** of our Terms of Service. ## 16. Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Payments

In P2P trades, payment is made **directly between users**, and OMAYA acts only as a facilitator and, where applicable, an escrow or asset-locking provider.

- A **buyer** must pay the **exact amount**, within the required time, using **only the payment method shown** in the confirmed order and a payment account **registered in the buyer's own name**.
- A **seller** must confirm that the correct payment has **actually been received** in their own account before releasing Digital Assets; a **screenshot, receipt, or notification is not proof of final receipt**.
- Submitting a **false or altered receipt**, or falsely marking an order as paid, is a serious breach and may lead to cancellation, account restriction, reporting, and legal action.
- If a payment is **reversed, recalled, or charged back after Digital Assets have been released**, the amount becomes a **debt owed to OMAYA or the seller**, and we may set it off, freeze balances, or take other lawful action.
- We may **hold or freeze the Digital Assets and funds involved in a P2P trade during a dispute** while we review the available evidence.

## 17. Set-Off and Negative Balances

We may **set off and deduct** from any balance in your Account (fiat or Digital Asset) any amount you owe us, including unpaid fees, negative balances, reversed or charged-back payments, duplicate credits, and amounts arising from your breach of our Terms. Where a balance is insufficient, you must **repay the shortfall on demand**, and we may convert Digital Assets to fiat (or the reverse) at prevailing rates to give effect to this Section.

## 18. Dormant-Account and Other Fees

Where permitted by law, we may charge a **dormant-account maintenance fee** on an Account that has had no login or transaction activity for an extended period, as described in our Terms of Service. We may also charge **administrative fees** for specific services, such as attempted recovery of misdirected or unsupported assets, as described in this Policy or on the Platform.

## 19. Payment Rules by Service

In addition to the general rules above, the following apply to specific Services:

- **Exchange (crypto ↔ local payment).** When you buy Digital Assets, they are credited after your local payment is received and cleared; when you sell Digital Assets, your local payment is sent after your Digital Assets are received and confirmed, at the rate confirmed at the time.
- **Swap (crypto ↔ crypto).** Swaps are priced at the rate confirmed at the time, may be routed through third-party liquidity providers, and are final once confirmed; network fees apply to each asset and network.
- **MoneyX (local payment transfers).** Transfers depend on the sending and receiving providers; the receiving account must match the beneficiary details you provide, and provider delays, outages, and reversal decisions are outside our control.
- **Remittance.** Payouts may be made by bank transfer, mobile money, or cash pickup where available, may involve agents, and may require the recipient to complete verification before payout; cancellation and refund depend on whether the funds have already been paid out, and any refund may be made at the prevailing exchange rate.
- **Forex (local agent).** OMAYA acts **only as a local agent and payment conduit** for third-party Brokers. The **Broker sets all spreads, commissions, and financing charges**, which are governed by the Broker's own terms. For deposits, our responsibility ends once funds reach the Broker; for withdrawals, it begins only on our receipt of funds from the Broker. See Section 7 of our Terms of Service.
- **P2P.** See Section 16.
