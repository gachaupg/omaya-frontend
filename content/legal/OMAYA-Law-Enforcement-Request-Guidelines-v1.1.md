# Law Enforcement Request Guidelines

**OMAYA EXPRESS EXCHANGE**

**Effective Date:** 31 May 2026  
**Last Updated:** 31 May 2026  
**Version:** 1.1

---

These guidelines explain how **OMAYA EXPRESS EXCHANGE** (trading as **OMAYA.io**) receives and responds to law enforcement and regulatory requests relating to our Services.

---

## 1. Scope

We cooperate with lawful requests from competent authorities where we are legally permitted and required to do so, consistent with applicable law and our privacy obligations.

## 2. How to Submit a Request

Requests should be sent from an official government or regulatory email domain and include:

- The requesting agency and jurisdiction
- Relevant case or reference numbers
- Specific data sought and time period
- Legal basis for the request
- Contact details for the authorised officer

## 3. User Notice

Where permitted by law, we may notify affected users before disclosing information, unless prohibited or where notice could compromise an investigation.

## 4. Emergency Requests

For imminent threat-to-life situations, clearly mark the request as urgent and include sufficient detail for us to assess and respond promptly.

## 5. Contact

Law enforcement and regulatory enquiries: **support@omaya.io** (subject line: Law Enforcement Request).

---

*We may update these guidelines from time to time.*
